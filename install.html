Put PFBeacon on your phone

Done. Look for the purple PFBeacon shield on your home screen.

Use Chrome. If no purple button shows above:

  1. Tap the 3 dots ⋮
    at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap “Install app”
    or “Add to Home screen”.
  3. Tap “Install” again.
    The purple PFBeacon shield is now on your home screen.
Please open this page in Safari (the blue compass), not in another app.
  1. Tap the Share button
    (the square with an arrow up) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Check “Open as Web App” is on (green), then tap “Add” at the top right.

Done. Open PFBeacon from your home screen, not from Safari.
