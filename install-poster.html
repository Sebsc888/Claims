To print: press Ctrl+P (or ⌘P), choose A4. The QR code points to the address INSTALL_URL in config.js. Change it there if the address changes.

Put the Claims app on your phone

1. Open your phone camera.
2. Point it at the square.
3. Tap the link that appears.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the big green Install button. (No button? Tap ⋮ at the top right, then Install app.)
  3. Open Claims from your home screen.

iPhone

  1. Open the link in Safari. Tap Share (square with arrow up).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App on. Tap Add. Open Claims from your home screen.
